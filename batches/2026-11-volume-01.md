## How to review

1. Open `rules/volumes/01-ghar.md` or `rules/rules.csv`.
2. Mark any rule you want changed by ID, e.g. **R-0042**.
3. Say what bothers you: title, twist, cast, or the whole story.
4. Approved rules move from `candidate` to `approved` in `rules/rules.csv`.
5. Rejected rules go to `ledger/rejected.csv` with a one-line reason.

IDs never change. Rewording a rule does not make it new.
