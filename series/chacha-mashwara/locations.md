# Chacha's Locations

Chacha tells every story from somewhere. The location is not decoration: it is the
first image of the episode and the last image after the verdict. One location per
episode. The city is unnamed; the locations are recognizable.

## Location rules

- Chacha is always seated or standing still. No walking-and-talking.
- One practical light source: a bulb, a lamp, a streetlight, a screen.
- The rulebook is visible in every frame.
- The location should feel lived-in, not designed.
- The first shot and the final shot are the same location. The story happens
  between them.
- No location is used twice within 20 episodes.
- Locations 16 (mosque courtyard) and 09 (graveyard gate) require care: no
  religious horror, no disrespect, no rituals. They are settings, not props.

## Location assignment

The build script rotates locations deterministically, avoiding repeats within 20
episodes. When writing a batch by hand, choose the location last: the rule should
suggest it, not the other way around.
