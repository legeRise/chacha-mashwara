# R-0001 — Never Answer the Door After Midnight

**Rule ID:** R-0001
**Runtime target:** 58 seconds
**Chacha location:** A tea stall before dawn (Location 01)
**Story cast:** HAMZA (V1), AYESHA (V2, phone voice), THE VOICE (V6)
**Chacha:** CH
**No narrator. No Chacha inside the story.**

---

## 0:00–0:09 — COLD OPEN

**Location:** A tea stall before dawn. Steam, cracked benches, one bulb.
Chacha sits with the rulebook closed on his lap.

**CH**
*(pause, looks at the viewer)*
"Suno meri baat. Aadhi raat ke baad darwaza kabhi mat kholna. Agar khola, toh jo
awaaz bahar hai woh tumhari awaaz seekh legi."

*He opens the rulebook. Camera pushes into the page.*

---

## 0:09–0:30 — THE STORY

**Location:** A second-floor flat above a tailor's shop. One lamp. A chain-locked
door.

**HAMZA** *(V1)* is alone on the sofa. His phone buzzes.

**AYESHA** *(V2, on phone)*
"Main das minute mein pohanch rahi hoon. Darwaza khol ke rakhna."

**HAMZA**
"Keys le li hain?"

**AYESHA**
"Haan. Bas darwaza kholna."

*She hangs up. Hamza puts the phone down. A knock at the door.*

**THE VOICE** *(V6, outside, using Ayesha's cadence)*
"Hamza. Darwaza kholo. Keys bhool gayi."

*Hamza freezes. The voice is Ayesha's.*

**HAMZA**
"Ayesha?"

**THE VOICE**
"Jaldi karo. Bahar bohat thand hai."

*Hamza checks his phone. Ayesha's location is still at the wedding hall. He calls
her. She picks up.*

**AYESHA** *(V2, on phone, annoyed)*
"Main abhi tak wedding mein hoon. Tum darwaza kyun nahi khol rahe? Main das minute
se knock kar rahi hoon."

*Hamza looks at the door. The chain is still on. The corridor light is off.*

**THE VOICE** *(V6, now using Hamza's laugh)*
"Ab andar aa sakta hoon?"

*Hamza steps back. The chain slides open by itself. The door opens. The corridor is
empty.*

---

## 0:30–0:52 — THE STORY, CONTINUED

*Hamza picks up the phone. The call to Ayesha is still connected.*

**AYESHA** *(V2, small)*
"Hamza? Tumhare ghar mein koi aur hai?"

*The voice comes from inside the flat now, from the kitchen.*

**THE VOICE** *(V6, using Hamza's voice)*
"Haan. Main hoon."

*Hamza turns. The kitchen light is on. A chair is pulled out. The phone falls from
his hand.*

---

## 0:52–0:58 — RETURN

**Same tea stall. Same shot. Chacha closes the rulebook.**

**CH**
*(pause)*
"Tum ne awaaz pehchani, darwaza nahi."

*He taps the cover twice.*

**CH**
"Rule yaad rakhna."

*Cut to black.*

---

## FINAL IMAGE

*The open door, the empty corridor, the phone glowing on the floor. Hold two
seconds. No music sting.*

---

## Production notes

- **Chacha frame:** tea stall set, one bulb, one bench, rulebook. Two angles.
- **Story sets:** one flat (sofa, door, corridor) + kitchen doorway. Keep the camera
  close.
- **Voices:** Hamza (V1), Ayesha (V2), The Voice (V6). The Voice must sound like
  Ayesha at first, then like Hamza. Same actor can do the shift; do not cast a
  separate "monster" voice.
- **Sound:** phone buzz, chain slide, kitchen chair. No jump-scare sting.
- **Thumbnail:** composition 2 (doorway/threshold); text **NEVER ANSWER**; Chacha
  corner mark.
- **Title:** Never Answer the Door After Midnight.
- **Description:** "Aadhi raat ke baad darwaza kabhi mat kholna. Chacha Mashwara ki
  rulebook se ek kahani." + rule + consequence.
