# Channel Constitution

## The promise

Every episode, Chacha Mashwara gives you one rule you should never break — and
then shows you what happened to the person who broke it.

The rule is the title. The story is the proof. The verdict is the payoff.

**Tagline:** *"Suno meri baat."*

## The audience

Urdu-speaking viewers, roughly 16–40, who grew up with warnings from elders and
still get a chill from ordinary things: a door, a mirror, a bus seat, a wedding
guest. They are not here for gore. They are here for the feeling of *"I have done
that"* — and the relief that they got away with it.

Secondary audience: Urdu diaspora viewers who want a Pakistani story world, not a
generic haunted house.

## The emotional core

**Dread + recognition.**

The horror comes from a small, understandable choice — not stupidity, not evil.
The viewer should think: *I might have done that too.* The consequence should feel
like a bill, not a punishment.

## Tone

- Warm, measured, adult.
- Eerie, not gruesome.
- Moral, not preachy.
- Pakistani everyday life, specific and lived-in.
- Chacha is warm but never a comedian. No jokes. No comedy engine.
- The story is serious. The only relief is Chacha's presence at the frame.

## The format

1. **Cold open** — Chacha in a location. He states the rule and the consequence.
2. **Transition** — he opens the rulebook; the camera enters the page.
3. **Story** — fully dramatized, 2–4 characters, dialogue only. Chacha never appears.
4. **Return** — Chacha delivers the verdict.
5. **Final image** — one silent shot from the story.

No narrator. No voice-over inside the story. Characters speak for themselves.

## The brand signature

- The worn leather rulebook. Always present, always closed at the end.
- Chacha's green waistcoat, cream prayer cap, and ring.
- A new location every episode. The city is unnamed but mapped.
- The final line is a verdict, not a moral lecture.
- The final image is silent and holds for two seconds.

## Hard NOs

- No gore, mutilation, or graphic death.
- No religious disrespect. No fake rituals, no misuse of prayers or scripture.
- No real names, real addresses, or identifiable living people.
- No sexual content.
- No children harmed on screen.
- No cruelty played for laughs.
- No explaining the supernatural. The rule is the explanation.

## Success metrics

Judge episodes by:

1. **Viewed vs. swiped away** (first 2 seconds).
2. **Average view duration** (does the story hold to the verdict?).
3. **Subscribers per video** (does the character make people return?).
4. **Comments naming the rule** (did the promise land?).
5. **Saves/shares** (would someone send this as a warning?).

Raw views are a lagging indicator. Consistency of promise is the leading one.

## The one rule of the channel

If an idea cannot be stated as **one rule**, it does not belong on this channel.
