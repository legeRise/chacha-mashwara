# R-0001 — Production Packet

**Episode:** Never Answer the Door After Midnight
**Runtime:** 58 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920
**Chacha:** tea stall before dawn
**Story cast:** Hamza, Ayesha (phone only), The Voice (never seen)
**No narrator. Chacha does not appear in the story.**

This packet turns the script into a finished video. Work in this order:
**voices → animatic → visuals → edit → thumbnail → publish.** Do not animate
before the voice track is locked; the timing will fight you.

---

## 1. Assets to build first

### Character anchors

**CHACHA** — already exists in Ezclip. Use the existing anchor.
Late fifties, round soft belly, grey moustache, thin round glasses, cream prayer
cap, white shalwar kameez, deep green waistcoat with gold buttons, one ring.

**HAMZA** — create once, reuse for the whole episode.
Pakistani young man, mid-20s, short black hair, light stubble, tired eyes, simple
dark kurta or plain t-shirt, no jewelry. He should look ordinary, not heroic.

**AYESHA** — voice only. No on-screen character needed.
If you need a visual, use a phone screen with her name/location pin. Do not show
her face; the story works better if the audience only hears her.

**THE VOICE** — never shown. No character design. It only exists as a voice, a
shadow under the door, and a chain moving.

### Sets

1. **Tea stall before dawn** — one yellow bulb, steam, cracked bench, dark blue
   pre-dawn street. Warm and safe.
2. **Flat interior** — sofa, one table lamp, chain-locked door, dark corridor
   beyond, kitchen doorway. Cramped. One or two camera angles.
3. **Kitchen doorway** — small table, one chair, bulb. Only seen at the end.

Keep the corridor darker than the flat. The only light sources are the phone
screen, the table lamp, and the kitchen bulb.

## 2. Voice track

Record or generate the voices first. Keep this exact order and timing.

| Time | Voice | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | CHACHA | “Suno meri baat.” | Warm, low, as if warning a friend. |
| 0:04–0:09 | CHACHA | “Aadhi raat ke baad darwaza kabhi mat kholna. Agar khola, toh jo awaaz bahar hai woh tumhari awaaz seekh legi.” | Pause before the rule. No drama. |
| 0:10–0:14 | AYESHA (phone) | “Main das minute mein pohanch rahi hoon. Darwaza khol ke rakhna.” | Ordinary, tired, slightly distracted. |
| 0:14–0:16 | HAMZA | “Keys le li hain?” | Small, practical. |
| 0:16–0:18 | AYESHA (phone) | “Haan. Bas darwaza kholna.” | Quick. She is about to hang up. |
| 0:18–0:21 | THE VOICE (outside) | “Hamza. Darwaza kholo. Keys bhool gayi.” | Perfect copy of Ayesha. Slightly muffled by the door. |
| 0:21–0:23 | HAMZA | “Ayesha?” | Confused, not scared yet. |
| 0:23–0:26 | THE VOICE (outside) | “Jaldi karo. Bahar bohat thand hai.” | Ayesha’s impatience, a little too calm. |
| 0:26–0:32 | AYESHA (phone) | “Main abhi tak wedding mein hoon. Tum darwaza kyun nahi khol rahe? Main das minute se knock kar rahi hoon.” | Annoyed. This is the proof. |
| 0:32–0:35 | THE VOICE (outside) | “Ab andar aa sakta hoon?” | Now using Hamza’s laugh. Quiet, amused. |
| 0:35–0:38 | AYESHA (phone) | “Hamza? Tumhare ghar mein koi aur hai?” | Small. She has heard something. |
| 0:38–0:41 | THE VOICE (inside) | “Haan. Main hoon.” | Hamza’s voice, from the kitchen. Dry, close. |
| 0:52–0:56 | CHACHA | “Tum ne awaaz pehchani, darwaza nahi.” | A sigh, not a punchline. |
| 0:56–0:58 | CHACHA | “Rule yaad rakhna.” | Soft. Then silence. |

**Voice treatment:**

- **Ayesha on phone:** high-pass filter, light compression, slight phone distortion.
- **The Voice outside:** same treatment as Ayesha, plus door muffling and a short
  room reverb. It must sound like her through a door, not like a monster.
- **The Voice inside:** remove the door effect. Dry, close, almost intimate.
- **The Voice using Hamza:** same actor as Hamza if possible, or a close imitation.
  Do not pitch it down.
- **Chacha:** no effects. Warm room. One breath before the verdict.

## 4. Shot prompts for Ezclip

Use these as image prompts / first-frame prompts. Keep the style consistent:
**2D animated illustration, Pakistani urban setting, clean line art, muted warm
palette, cinematic lighting, 9:16 vertical.** No text in the image.

1. **Tea stall wide** — “2D animated cinematic still, pre-dawn Pakistani street tea stall, one hanging yellow bulb, steam from a kettle, cracked wooden bench, an older Pakistani uncle in a green waistcoat and cream prayer cap seated with a closed leather book on his lap, warm amber light, dark blue pre-dawn background, clean line art, muted palette, 9:16.”
2. **Chacha close-up** — “Close-up of a Pakistani uncle in his late fifties, grey moustache, thin round glasses, cream prayer cap, white shalwar kameez, deep green waistcoat, kind tired eyes, warm bulb light from below, dark tea stall background, serious expression, no smile, 9:16.”
3. **Book transition** — “Macro shot of weathered hands opening an old leather rulebook, blank aged pages, dust motes in warm lamplight, dark background, no text, 9:16.”
4. **Hamza on sofa** — “2D animated Pakistani flat interior at night, young man mid-20s with short black hair and light stubble sitting on a sofa, phone to his ear, one table lamp, chain-locked door in the background, cramped room, muted warm palette, 9:16.”
5. **Phone down** — “Close-up of a young man’s hand placing a phone on a wooden table, lamp light, shallow depth of field, quiet dark room, 9:16.”
6. **Door and chain** — “Low-angle shot of a chain-locked door in a dim Pakistani flat, shadow of feet under the door, corridor light off, one lamp behind, tense, 9:16.”
7. **Peephole** — “Extreme close-up of a door peephole, distorted dark empty corridor beyond, a young man’s eye silhouette at the edge of frame, 9:16.”
8. **Phone location** — “Close-up of a phone screen showing a location pin at a wedding hall, young man’s thumb hovering, lamp light, no readable text, 9:16.”
9. **Chain opens** — “Close-up of a door chain sliding open by itself, door swinging into a dark empty corridor, no person, cold corridor light, 9:16.”
10. **Kitchen** — “Pakistani flat interior, kitchen doorway, bulb snapping on, empty chair pulled out at a small table, phone on the floor in the foreground, no person, 9:16.”
11. **Phone falls / final image** — “Low-angle shot of a phone falling from a hand, screen glowing, kitchen light, motion blur; cut to an open door, empty corridor, phone glowing on the floor, 9:16.”
12. **Return** — “Pre-dawn tea stall, same framing as the opening, the older uncle closes the leather rulebook, warm yellow bulb, steam, dark blue street, quiet, 9:16.”

**Do not generate:** the entity, a ghost, a monster, a face in the corridor, red eyes,
or a jump-scare image. The horror is the chain moving and the chair pulled out.

## 5. Edit

1. Put the voice track on the timeline first.
2. Cut the visuals to the voice; do not let a shot run past its line.
3. Add sound effects:
   - phone buzz (0:10)
   - knock (0:17)
   - chain slide (0:28)
   - kitchen bulb click (0:38)
   - chair scrape (0:40)
   - phone drop (0:45)
4. Silence after the phone drops. No music sting.
5. Add a low room tone under the flat scenes. The tea stall has street ambience.
6. Add Urdu subtitles only if they help; keep them small and out of the thumbnail
   area.
7. Final shot: hold two seconds on the open door/corridor/phone. Then cut to black.
8. Export 1080×1920, 30fps.

strip of light. Chacha in the corner mark. No face, no ghost.

**Text:** **NEVER ANSWER** (2 words, heavy sans, cream on deep green/oxblood).

**Prompt:** “2D animated thumbnail, a chain-locked door ajar into a dark empty
corridor, warm flat light behind, cold darkness beyond, a small silhouette of an
older Pakistani uncle in the corner, cinematic, high contrast, no text, 9:16.”
